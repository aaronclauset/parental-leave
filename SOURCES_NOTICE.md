# Source documents and quotations

The structured encodings in `records/` quote short passages of the documents they encode: faculty handbooks, collective agreements, university policies, human-resources pages and statutes. Each quote is the evidence for one coded field, included so that every value can be checked against its source.

The copyright in those documents remains with their owners. The quotations are short excerpts, used for research and verification. The documents themselves are not distributed in this repository. `provenance/index.csv` identifies each one by its original URL and, where one exists, a web-archive capture.
