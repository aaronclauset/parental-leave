# Coding checklist

This is the reader's checklist for encoding one institution's parental-leave documents into a structured record. It is version 1.25, the version under which the 2018 data set v2.0 was produced. It is read with the interpretive rules (`rules.md`), the schema (`schema.md`), the field meanings (`field_meanings.md`) and the reader instructions (`reader_instructions.md`), which share its version number.

Throughout, **v1.0** means the earlier data set: the faculty parental-leave data published with Morgan, Way, Galesic, Larremore and Clauset (2018), with its corrections through January 2019. This release (**v2.0**) re-collects the same institutions from the policies in force in the 2017–18 academic year.

The same guidance governs later collections (a 2026 collection is under way); the examples are from the 2018 data. Published with the data: the records, this guidance, `programs.csv`, `statutory_baseline.csv`, the 2017–18 calendar table (`calendar_2017_18.csv`), the code and the provenance index. Not published: the source documents and saved statute texts (identified by URL and web-archive capture in the provenance index), the passage index, the readers' reports, and the checker the readers ran (`check_extraction.py`), which tests a record against the saved source texts.

**How to use it.** Work through §1 once per institution, then §2 once per component, field by field, then §3 once per record. §5 gives worked examples of the hardest structures.
- Each line asks one question and gives the default answer. Where your case is not the default, open the rules the line names in `rules.md` and code by them.
- Every judgment gets one entry in the component's decision log (§4).
- The schema (`schema.md`) defines every field's format and codes.
- **Read each provision whole:** with its implementing paragraphs, definitions and exceptions. A specific provision governs a general statement of the same benefit (source precedence). Where two governing documents in force on the reference date disagree, decide the reading by V3d.

## 1. What to extract: scope classes (search every source for each class)

**Before you code any benefit, settle whose figures these are.** The values describe one main-campus tenure-stream member with two years of service, eligible for the statutory benefits the documents name; the statutory floor of the jurisdiction applies in any case (U7), taking each leave as written; where the documents set paths by eligibility or residence, code that member's path and put the others in notes (U8). Never link two paths' benefits as `in_lieu_of` each other: the derivation would mix them.

**Could a parent use it?** Before applying U1 or `other`, ask whether a parent could take it for the birth or adoption. Leave for a death, a wedding, a move, jury service or domestic violence cannot be; it is not a component (Tufts, Ottawa special leaves). A leave for "any purpose" or "personal reasons" can be: a component with `general_purpose` (U1). An accommodation for any disability (adaptive equipment) is not a component (Yale); a pregnancy accommodation is (`other`, R16).

Record every provision in these classes that covers main-campus tenure-stream faculty, and those that cover other groups or exclude them (`faculty_group` says who). For each class, the record's `scope_accounting` lists the component ids, or `{"absent": {"documents": [...], "terms": [...]}}`. Every component appears in at least one class.

| Class | What it is | Mechanism (usual) | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|

| C2 Relief from duties | Modified duties, teaching relief, course release for new parents | `modified_duties`; a rearrangement is `other` | R3–R3f, R4–R4c, R16 |

| C4 Statutes and government programs | FMLA; state leave acts; state paid programs; EI, QPIP; provincial employment standards. **Account for every statute named anywhere in the documents** (for an Act, the sections the documents rely on, and those extending them, are components; its other sections are listed with the reason: U4): a component where U4 applies; otherwise listed in the class's entry with the reason it is not one (U4). ## 2. Per component, field by field

| Field | Question | Default | Open when not the default |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Split first** | Is this one provision? | One provision, one component | Different provisions, mechanisms, payers or alternatives: split and link (V4). A length that differs by role: one component with `duration_by_role`. A stated envelope that includes employer-paid waiting weeks: one component (V4) |
| `component_id`, `institution_label`, `description` | Identity | `<slug>_cN`; the policy's own name verbatim, or `""`; your summary in `description` | Schema |
| `mechanism` | What kind of program is it? | As named in the text | A childbirth period through a sick or disability plan: V1, V1b, R2. A program in `programs.csv`: the table's mechanism (M1). A paid medical leave of absence for the birth parent's own disability: the leave (`unpaid_leave`) plus its pay (generic plan `within` it), only where the text grants the absence (U6). A sick-pay allotment the employer restores: R1. A general sick-day allowance usable for paternity, adoption and family care is not a childbirth-specific period: `own_accrual` (V1). A rearrangement or accommodation: R16. Hazard or equal treatment: U4. A state program: R14, M1. A reduced appointment with pay in proportion to duties: `part_time_option` (R3). Long-term disability: schema §2 |
| `payer` | Who pays? | Leave with no pay: `none`. Pay the text says the institution provides: `employer` | Only the employee's own balance: R1, R1b, R1c, R1d. Insurance the employee buys: `employee_paid` (R1d). A government benefit, or an insurer with funding unstated: M1. A colleague-funded bank: R1d |
| Purpose: `general_purpose` flag | Are any of its stated purposes outside health, family care, a birth or an adoption? | No flag | U1 (open lists, tenure clocks for any leave, the current purpose; a closed list of birth, adoption, health, family care or a death in the family is not general; never sick, disability or FMLA-event pay) |
| `faculty_group`, `faculty_scope` | Does the covered group include main-campus tenure-stream faculty? | The code `main_campus_tenure_stream`, with the policy's words in `faculty_group_note` | One college or school: V3, V3b. Unit and university policies differ: V3c. A group defined by appointment length that leaves out academic-year faculty: name it (schema §2b) |
| `eligible_parent`, `caregiver_condition` | Which parents can use it because of a birth or adoption? | The roles the text names | Gendered words, surrogacy: R5, R6. Own illness or disability: `birth_parent`, flag `own_illness_leave`; care of an ill family member: `non_birth_parent`, flag `family_illness_leave`; both purposes: both, with both flags (U5). A leave with no stated end that is not only the employee's own illness (a pregnancy or childbirth disability leave; a medical leave that serves both self and family care): flag `open_ended_medical_leave`, counted to week 52 for each parent it serves (U5); not for leaves that end before the birth. The text's role and caregiver words: `eligible_parent_wording`, `caregiver_condition_wording` |
| `entitlement` | What does the approval decide? | The text grants it outright ("shall", "is entitled", "will receive"): `guaranteed`. The text gives it and approval covers only the arrangements: `guaranteed_on_request` | "May be granted", "at the discretion": `discretionary` (V2). "Should": `discretionary` (V2c). "Unless", "normally granted", "not unreasonably denied": `guaranteed_on_request` with a `conditional_refusal` flag (V2b, V2d), except where the text also disclaims an entitlement: `discretionary` (V2d). Amount or form to be agreed: R12. A statute: `guaranteed`. Required on every component |
| `duration` | How long, as written? | Value, unit and qualifier as stated (R13) | Qualifiers: R13, R10, Q1. Ranges, either-or and conditional lengths: R13b (`duration_max`). Units: R11, R11b, R8. A statute named without its length; EI's options: U2. The conditions behind a range in `duration_variants`; the reasoning in `duration_note` |
| `duration_by_role`, `paid_duration` | Do the terms differ by role, or is only part paid? | Absent | Schema entries for each |
| `job_protected_weeks` | Does the text guarantee return to the job for the whole leave? | Absent (the whole leave is protected) | The job is held for only part of the leave: the weeks guaranteed. The text disclaims reemployment: `0`. The standard narrow exceptions (FMLA key employees, a position eliminated, a statute's own exceptions) do not count (U3) |
| `must_exhaust_first` | Must the employee use balances before this applies? | Absent | List them (sick, vacation, compensatory). Whether the employer still guarantees the period: R1c, R1d, R1e |
| `medical_certification`, `medical_certification_note` | Is a medical certification required? | Absent | `true`/`false`, with what is certified |
| `dual_employee_rule` | Are both parents employed by the institution treated differently? | Absent | As written ("combined 12 weeks", "each may take") |

## 4. Decision log (`decision_log`)

One entry for each judgment: a value decided by a rule or a reading, not copied from the text. The judgment fields are those listed for `evidence` (schema §2f), plus `mechanism`, `payer`, the `general_purpose` flag (whether set or not), relief inferred under R4b or R9, and `sequence`.
```
{"field": "...", "rule": "U1", "answer": "...", "quote": "<deciding quote, verbatim>"}
```
- Where several rules decide one field, one entry per rule.
- `rule` is a rule id, or `schema` or `source_precedence`.
- Every field, answer and quote is non-empty. `field` is the record or component field decided, `general_purpose` or `leave_limits`.
- For `mechanism`, `entitlement`, `payer`, `caregiver_condition`, `faculty_group` and `general_purpose` (`true`/`false`), the answer must equal the value coded.
- The checker requires an entry for every judgment field that is filled.

## 5. Worked examples

1. **Leave, employer top-up and EI (Canada).** "A Member … is entitled to a parental leave with allowance … not exceeding eighteen (18) weeks"; the allowance tops EI up to 95%. Three components:
   - the leave: `unpaid_leave`, payer `none`, 18 weeks `up_to`, `guaranteed`;
   - the allowance: `employer_topup`, `within` the leave. Where it is paid only while EI is received ("the difference between the EI parental benefits he/she is in receipt of and …"), `start_offset_weeks` 1, like EI; its duration stays as written (18, `up_to`) and it ends with the leave (R15);
   - EI parental: `government_benefit`, `concurrent_with` the leave, `start_offset_weeks` 1 (R21). An employer schedule that pays the first weeks itself ("100% of salary for the first two weeks") changes what the employer pays, not when EI starts.

   `unpaid_leave` names the leave itself, not a claim that income is zero. Where the statutory leave is the only parental leave (BC), the top-up and the employer's waiting-week pay are linked `within` the statutory leave (R18, S1).
2. **A stale waiting period.** See R21's Saskatchewan and Manitoba examples (the statute sets when EI starts; the agreement sets what the employer pays).
3. **EI's extended option.** U2: the standard option counts; the extended one goes in `duration_variants`.
4. **Medical leave after balances run out.** "An employee unable to return to work after exhausting all accumulated sick leave … may be granted" up to a year of unpaid sick leave: `unpaid_leave`, `birth_parent` (own illness, U5, flag `own_illness_leave`), `discretionary`, `consecutive_after` the sick-leave component where the text places it so. A balance of unknown length adds no weeks.
5. **An old title.** U1's Purdue example: the current purpose governs; the old newborn-care leave is not a component.
6. **General and specific paragraphs.** "Shall be granted upon approval" in one paragraph, "may be granted" and grounds for rejection in the next: read together, `discretionary` (source precedence).
7. **A partial-relief alternative with no stated amount.** A semester of full relief "or" an unquantified reduction in teaching and service: two components linked `in_lieu_of`; the partial option has `partially_relieved` where named, no `duration`, the span in `relief_note` (R3c).
8. **A US state paid-leave program that protects the job.** New York Paid Family Leave (2018: 8 weeks, partial pay, job-protected): a `government_benefit` (payer by M1) and an `unpaid_leave` for the protected 8 weeks, linked `concurrent_with`; the leave carries no pay. Where the program pays but does not protect the job (a disability benefit), only the `government_benefit`. A cap shared by two named benefits only ("combined … 26 weeks"): a `paid_caps` entry naming the two components, not `cap_weeks_all_sources` (R20, M1).
9. **A stated envelope against stages.** A stated envelope is one component ("95% of salary for up to 35 weeks, including the waiting period": Saskatchewan, V4). Stages stated separately are separate components (Ottawa: "95% … for the first ten working days", then the top-up to 95% with EI for up to 15 weeks; UBC: one waiting week at 95%, then 15 weeks of top-up). Do not merge stages into an envelope the text does not state. Where a later note changes how a stage falls after a statutory change, the later note governs.
