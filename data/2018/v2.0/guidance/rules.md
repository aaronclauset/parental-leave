# Interpretive rules

These rules decide how a faculty parental-leave policy is read into a structured record, and how the record's components count toward the values computed from it. The schema (`schema.md`), the coding checklist (`coding_checklist.md`), the field meanings (`field_meanings.md`) and the reader instructions (`reader_instructions.md`) are read with them. This is version 1.25, the version under which the 2018 data set v2.0 was produced.

Throughout, **v1.0** means the earlier data set: the faculty parental-leave data published with Morgan, Way, Galesic, Larremore and Clauset (2018), with its corrections through January 2019. This release (**v2.0**) re-collects the same institutions from the policies in force in the 2017–18 academic year.

The same guidance governs later collections (a 2026 collection is under way); the examples are from the 2018 data. Published with the data: the records, this guidance, `programs.csv`, `statutory_baseline.csv`, the 2017–18 calendar table (`calendar_2017_18.csv`), the code and the provenance index. Not published: the source documents and saved statute texts (identified by URL and web-archive capture in the provenance index), the passage index, the readers' reports, and the checker the readers ran (`check_extraction.py`), which tests a record against the saved source texts.

Rules are grouped by what they govern. Each has an identifier, which records cite in their decision logs, the rule, and worked examples from the institutions' own documents. Rule ids are labels: their order in this file and gaps in the numbering carry no meaning. In the rules, the **2018-definition figure** (the primary value) means weeks of paid leave or relief as v1.0 defined them: employer-paid leave or relief from duties, whichever is longer (R3).

## 1. Pay: what counts as paid, and how much

| Id | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|

A pool that colleagues fund is coded `donated_leave` when the documents present it as its own program, and inside `own_accrual` when it is part of the employee's own balance rules. | V1 | A childbirth period paid through a general sick or disability plan is coded `employer_childbirth_benefit` when the documents state all three of: (i) a **childbirth-specific** period, (ii) a length or default length, and (iii) a pay level. Hawaii TDI met through the UH faculty sick-leave policy (Appendix T) → `employer`, `concurrent_with` the sick-leave component |
| R16 | Paid time during which teaching **and** service are still required is not leave. A pregnancy accommodation that changes the position or the work, without relief from teaching or service, is coded `other` | Florida State UFF 17.7(a): paid parental leave of up to 6 months, full relief from teaching and service capped at one semester → 1 semester. TTIC: a quarter free of classroom teaching with the year's full load still taught → `other`. UC Berkeley, transfer "to a less strenuous or hazardous position" → `other` |
| R17 | A general rule that pays the first days of any FMLA-qualifying absence as regular work time counts as employer-paid leave for both parents, because a faculty member who becomes a parent receives it | New Mexico State ARP 8.42: the first five work days of an FMLA event are paid as regular work time → 1 week, both roles |

## 2. Relief from duties

| Id | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| R3 | When full relief is **followed by** partial relief, code the longer, combined period (see R3b, R3c, R3f). "Partial relief" means relief from some duties at **full pay**; relief or leave that comes with a pay cut smaller than the reduction in duties is coded as relief or leave with its pay level (`pay_fraction_pct`). It counts in the 2018-definition figure at its full length, and its pay level enters the full-pay-equivalent figure and R15b's comparison of alternatives; it is not combined with other relief periods (R3f); a reduced appointment whose pay falls in proportion to its duties is coded `part_time_option` with its pay level recorded, and is not counted or combined. **Relief with no stated pay:** relief from duties for which the documents state no pay level, and no reduction, is read as relief at full pay. It counts under R3, and the full-pay-equivalent figure counts it at full pay (schema §3). The pay fields stay empty, since there is no text to quote, and the component is flagged `ambiguous: pay level not stated; full pay inferred (R3)`, so a sensitivity analysis can exclude it. Where any document states the pay, the pay is coded from that text | Penn State, Berkeley (birth parents). Brown (salary continues, no level stated) → flagged; Alabama–Huntsville ("all salary paid during the modified duties period": salary is paid, at no stated level) → flagged |
| R3b | Where relief covers the rest of the semester or term in which a leave falls, and is coded with the semester as its length, the leave is linked `within` the relief. The wording varies: "the remainder of the semester", "the balance of the semester", "the portion of the semester not covered by" the leave, "extended to the end of a semester". The relief period for the 2018-definition figure is the whole semester (leave + remainder). The same reading applies to an unpaid leave offered for the rest of the semester | Wayne State XIII.D.2.b → one semester, "Just teaching" (discretionary: with discretionary provisions only); Penn State 2018 → 16 (one semester, academic calendar); Boston University → 16 |
| R3c | Options offered as alternatives (e.g. 100% relief for one semester **or** 50% over two) are coded by the amount of relief, with the span in the note, and linked `in_lieu_of`. Consistent with v1.0's coding of George Mason (16 weeks, All duties). On the timeline (R15) the relief is placed at its relief-equivalent length; the span stays in the note. An alternative whose amount of relief is not stated is coded with its relief fields, no `duration`, and the span in `relief_note`; it adds no weeks and does not win R15b against a stated alternative. This no-`duration` clause is only for an alternative whose relief is unstated, not for a sole granted period, which keeps its `duration` (ASU). | George Mason c2: 1 semester of relief, spread over 2 semesters. Delaware 2018: a primary caregiver chooses a semester of full relief or an unquantified partial reduction → the full semester counts; the partial option is recorded with no `duration` |
| R3d | Relief that follows a paid leave, and does not include it, is linked `consecutive_after` the leave. Relief that a parent may take as well as the leave, with no order stated, is linked `also_available`. In both cases the 2018-definition figure is leave + relief, measured on the timeline (R15), in the relief's category (R3's combined period). Relief that contains the leave is R3b. The components stay separate, so other aggregations remain possible | Rutgers XVI.A–B: 6 weeks recuperative leave + up to 8 weeks release, "for a total of up to fourteen weeks" → 14, Just teaching. Penn 2018: relief that "will commence within six months of the baby's birth", after the 8-week leave → 8 + 17 = 25 |
| R3f | Full-pay relief periods linked to each other (`consecutive_after` or `also_available`) form one period: the 2018-definition figure is the time they, and any paid leave linked to them (R3b, R3d), cover together, measured on the timeline (R15), in the relief's category. A relief period `within` another adds nothing. Relief with a pay cut is never combined (R3) | Princeton: birth-semester workload relief (containing the childbearing leave) plus a separate new-parent semester → 2 semesters, Just teaching. |
| R4 | Relief is recorded per duty: teaching, service and administration, research, advising and supervision, clinical. The 2018-definition figure's category "Just teaching" = teaching relieved and research continuing; otherwise "Other"; a leave = "All duties". Refined by R4b and R4c | MIT teaching relief → "Just teaching" |
| R4b | When a relief provision names only some duties, the duties it does not name are coded `continues`, with `relief_inferred: "unnamed duties continue (R4b)"` | Caltech: "full relief, with pay, from classroom teaching and administrative duties" → research `continues` (inferred) → "Just teaching". Listed alternate duties that exclude teaching make the period teaching relief (ASU ACD 710). |
| R4c | A paid leave whose text releases only some duties while research continues (stated, or unnamed under R4b) takes its relief category from its relief fields, as modified duties do (R4): teaching relieved with research continuing is "Just teaching". A paid leave that states no relief (R9) or that relieves research stays "All duties". At a research university research is a core duty, so a leave that keeps it is relief, not a full leave | GWU Faculty Code VI.D: one semester "released from teaching and service" at full salary, research unnamed (continues, R4b) → 14 weeks, Just teaching (v1.0 coded the category as Other). Michigan Tech paid semester, teaching relieved → Just teaching |
| R9 | A formal leave of absence whose relief is not stated is coded as relieved for every duty, with `relief_inferred: "formal leave"` | Simon Fraser parental leave |

## 3. Lengths and units

| Id | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| R8 | A term's length in weeks is, in order: (1) a length a source states (`calendar.term_weeks`, with the quote in the note); (2) the institution's academic calendar for the reference year: the first day of classes to the last day of exams, inclusive, less recesses of three or more weekdays (reading and study periods stay in the term), for the longest regular term of the year, rounded up to whole weeks; (3) the term length in v1.0, where it has one; (4) the default for the kind of term the sources name (`calendar.kind`: semester 16, quarter 11, term 16). A pay or appointment period is not a term length. Where a document states the kind of term but not its length, `calendar.term_weeks` is left empty and the kind is recorded in `calendar.kind`. Where a text gives a length for both quarter and semester calendars ("two quarters (or one semester)"), the alternative for the institution's calendar is coded, by this rule's order (UCLA 2018: APM 760, quarters) | Caltech quarters → 11 weeks; Wisconsin's 19.5-week pay semester and the Florida UFF agreement's "approximately 19.5 week" semester are not term lengths. Drexel: 'academic terms' that HR-33 shows are quarters → 11 weeks |
| R10 | "At least" minimums are coded as the amount, with qualifier `at_least` | Penn State |
| R11 | Conversions: 40 hours = 1 week; 5 days = 1 week; a month = 52/12 weeks; a year = 52 weeks | Oregon State 2018: 60 hours → 1.5 weeks |
| R11b | "Calendar days" convert at 7 per week (unit `calendar_day`); working days and plain "days" at 5 per week (R11). Course reductions are not converted to weeks: they are recorded as relief with no week count | South Carolina Faculty Manual: 180 calendar days → 25.7 weeks; Southern California 9-C(2) two-course reduction → not converted |
| R13 | Durations are coded exactly as written, with the qualifier (`exact`, `up_to`, `at_least`, `normally`). Ranges go in `duration_max` (R13b); the conditions of variant lengths go in `duration_variants`. Refined by Q1 and R13b. The qualifier records a qualifying word in the text: a length stated as a total with no qualifying word ('a total of 12 workweeks') is `exact`; 'or on an earlier day', 'or earlier', 'not to exceed' and 'up to' are qualifying words → `up_to`. | Ottawa 2018 art. 29.2.3.2: a leave that ends 17 weeks after it began 'or on an earlier day' on notice → `up_to` |
| R13b | A stated range or either-or ("six to eight weeks", "6 or 8 weeks") is coded as its lower value with qualifier `at_least`, and the upper value in `duration_max`. `duration_max` holds that upper value or, for a benefit that grows with service, the band beyond the two-year member's (U8). The derivation reports the 2018-definition figure at the lower value and `weeks_2018def_max` at the upper, so coverage can be reported as a floor and a ceiling. (v1.0 took the upper value.) A length that depends on a stated condition (vaginal or Cesarean delivery; leave before the birth that requires a finding of disability) is coded the same way: the shorter as `duration`, the longer (with the conditional time) as `duration_max`, and the conditions in `duration_variants`. Where each length is itself stated "up to", the qualifier is `up_to`. The protected-weeks figure also takes the lower value of a range | Mississippi State "normally six to eight weeks" → 6 at_least, max 8. DePaul "6 or 8 weeks of short-term disability" → `duration` 6 at_least, `duration_max` 8. Pace: 6 weeks (vaginal) or 8 (Cesarean) → 6 at_least, max 8. Santa Clara: 6 or 8 weeks after the birth, plus up to 4 weeks before it when disabled → 6, max 12. UCF: up to 6 weeks after the birth, up to 8 after a C-section → 6 up_to, max 8 |
| Q1 | "Normally/usually not more than X" and "not normally exceeding X" → qualifier `up_to`, with "normally" kept in the note | MIT medical leave "up to, but not normally exceeding, six months" → `up_to` 6 months |

## 4. Timing: how components combine

| Id | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| R15 | Paid components for the same parent run one after another unless the documents place them otherwise. A component `concurrent_with` or `within` another starts when that one starts, or `start_offset_weeks` later; a component `within` another also ends no later than it does; a component `consecutive_after` another starts when that one ends. A stated window that puts a component inside another's period is such a placement and is coded as a link (checklist `time_window`; Ottawa 2018 29.2.1: the two paid days within 30 days of the birth → `concurrent_with` the pregnancy leave; the non-birth parent's parental leave `consecutive_after` them). A window that says only when something may start or be claimed places nothing by itself. A start tied to the end of another of the record's leaves is a link, not a window, even when it is stated as a date: `consecutive_after` that leave; where the text names several leaves, the last of them the parent takes (Concordia 2018 Art. 35.14 m: sick leave benefits 'as of the expected date of return' after a maternity, paternity or parental leave → `consecutive_after` the parental leave). A window never replaces a link the text states. A placing link is a `concurrent_with`, `within` or `consecutive_after` link, which fixes a component's start relative to another; `also_available` places the component after the other; `in_lieu_of` marks alternatives. A component linked `also_available` with another, or with no placing link, runs after it (the longest-leave convention). Where a component runs alongside two leaves that are also available to each other, the second leave starts when the first ends; they stay added. A component that is `consecutive_after` several others starts when the last of them ends; it does not set their start, and any of them that nothing else places run one after another, ending where it starts. Where a component is placed by more than one relation, `consecutive_after` places it first, then a relation with `start_offset_weeks`; any other `concurrent_with` or `within` then states only that the two overlap. | McGill 20 + 11 |
| R18 | The paid figures place the paid components by their links to any component of the record, counted in the figure or not (a top-up and a benefit both linked to an unpaid leave start from that leave); a statute linked `concurrent_with` with no offset runs alongside them and does not place them. A component `within` a statute, and a statute placed by `consecutive_after` or by an offset, place like any other link (UBC 2018, top-ups `within` the ESA pregnancy and parental leaves → birth paid 51; UCLA 2018, discretionary paid FMLA `within` FMLA, which runs alongside the childbearing leave → 12). | Michigan State, relief after both parental leaves → 6 + 6 + 16 = 28 (discretionary variant). |
| R19 | The paid total is the time covered by at least one paid component: overlapping time counts once, and for full-pay-equivalent weeks at the highest pay level then in effect. | McGill 20 + 11 (R15) |
| R20 | An all-sources cap (`cap_weeks_all_sources`) limits the paid figures (the 2018-definition figure, full-pay-equivalent weeks and the paid total), not protected weeks; only a cap stated by a component counted for the role and variant applies. A stated limit on leave time (`leave_limits`) caps the time its listed components cover, in the paid figures and in protected weeks. A cap on all leaves for one event (a `leave_limits` entry) covers every leave for that event, medical leave included, unless the text excludes it (UCLA 2018: APM 760-35-b's one year covers APM 710 medical leave). A stated cap on the paid weeks of two or more named benefits together (`paid_caps`; not all paid sources) limits the paid figures for those components only, not leave time or protected weeks. A leave limit that the institution may waive at its discretion (`waivable_at_discretion`) binds the guaranteed figures only. | New Brunswick, combined maximum → 52. |
| R21 | **Waiting periods:** the length of each program's waiting period at the reference date is in `programs.csv`, with its statute and quote. The link is `concurrent_with` the leave, or a top-up that starts with the leave, with `start_offset_weeks` N. The reader codes `start_offset_weeks` from the table where the rule below places a wait, and the checker compares the coded waiting offsets with the table. A blank table value is coded from the statute, as below. Whether and where a wait applies is the reader's coding, by these rules: a government benefit whose first week or days the statute never pays (an unpaid waiting period: EI) starts after that period. A waiting period the statute pays back once benefits continue (NJ TDI and FLI 2018, N.J.S.A. 43:21-38: the first seven days are paid once benefits are payable for three consecutive weeks; the first week of family leave once benefits become payable after the first three weeks), or waives, delays nothing: no `start_offset_weeks`, with the repayment or waiver in the link's note. An employer that pays the unpaid week itself (a top-up) does not change when the benefit starts. The waiting period is counted from the start of the leave, and the statute sets it, whatever schedule the institution's text describes: `start_offset_weeks` equal to the statute's waiting period at the reference date (EI: one week from 2017-01-01, EI Act s. 13), on the benefit's link to the leave, or to a top-up that starts with the leave. A top-up that itself starts only after employer-paid waiting weeks does not move the benefit: link the benefit to the leave. The institution's text sets what the employer pays, as written, even where it describes an older waiting period; an employer term conditional on a statutory fact ('where EI imposes a two-week waiting period') applies only if the fact holds on the reference date. The waiting period is served once per birth, so a benefit that follows another (EI parental after EI maternity) has no second offset. The non-birth parent's figures assume no claim by the other parent: the non-birth parent serves its own waiting period (EI: `start_offset_weeks` 1); the deferral the statute allows where the other parent has served it (EI Act s. 23(5)) goes in the link's note. A waiting period with no second component to anchor it goes in `duration_note`, with `ambiguous: restriction: waiting period`. | Queen's 20-week maternity pay (weeks 1–2 salary, then a top-up; written for the old two-week wait): EI maternity `start_offset_weeks` 1 on its link to the leave, EI parental `consecutive_after` EI maternity → 51. Carleton: two weeks at 95% salary, then a 15-week top-up during which the employee claims EI → EI linked to the leave, `start_offset_weeks` 1, not to the top-up that starts in week 3 → 51. Saskatchewan 2018: a supplement of 35 weeks 'including the two-week benefit waiting period' → the supplement 35 weeks, EI one week after the leave starts. Manitoba 2018: two extra paid weeks 'where EI imposes a two-week waiting period' → do not apply; an allowance paid only while EI is received, up to 18 weeks within an 18-week leave → `within` the leave, `start_offset_weeks` 1, so it ends with the leave (17 weeks) |
| R15b | An option offered as a package of components (pay `within` a leave, as one choice among others) is linked `in_lieu_of` on the option's leave and on each of its paid components, not only on one: the derivation does not carry an alternative through `within` or `consecutive_after`. A component granted whichever option is chosen is not part of the option and carries no `in_lieu_of`: pay so granted, a statutory leave (S1, U7), and a leave that relief contains (R3b) or follows (R3d). Link such pay to a component every option shares (the statute, or the leave the options start from), not to one option's leave, which drops out when that option is not the one counted (Tufts 2018 option (a): 2 paid weeks `within` 10 unpaid weeks, instead of the 12-week paid leave → the unpaid leave and the 2 paid weeks each `in_lieu_of` the 12-week leave). Among alternatives (`in_lieu_of`), the 2018-definition figure takes the alternative with the most **full-pay-equivalent** weeks (weeks × `pay_fraction_pct` / 100); ties go to the full-pay alternative. Alternatives that depend on the timing of the birth, not on a choice, are compared the same way: the longest counts (Tufts 2018: relief normally covers the balance of the semester of the leave; after a mostly-summer leave it falls in the following semester → 12 + 16 = 28). **Relief alternatives** are compared by the relief period each gives once placed in time with the leaves it is linked to (R3b, R3d, R15), and the longest is kept; ties are settled by pay as above. Paid alternatives keep the comparison by their own weeks, because placing them would favor an alternative whose removal leaves a benefit linked to it unplaced. A separate derived column, `fullpay_equiv_weeks`, reports the pay-weighted total for every institution; the 2018-definition figure itself is never pay-weighted (R7: top-up weeks count as weeks) | Rochester 2018: 6 to 8 weeks at full pay (6, max 8); the half-pay semester option is stated only in the FAQ (`summary_only`) → 6 (max 8), "All duties". Penn State 2018: the birth-semester release (contains the 6-week leave, 16) vs the following-semester release (after it, 6 + 16 = 22) → 22, with discretionary provisions |
| S1 | Statutory job-protected leave that overlaps the institution's own paid leave (FMLA, CFRA, state acts) is coded `concurrent_with` that leave, with the unpaid remainder in the note; never `consecutive_after`, except where the institution's own text places the statutory leave after the paid leave (then `consecutive_after`, with the text in the note; New Mexico's faculty handbook section C215 2.4) or S1a applies. Universities commonly require faculty to take FMLA formally even while they are paid under the university's own policy. A statutory leave linked `concurrent_with` or `within` several of the institution's leaves starts when the earliest of them starts, unless an offset or S1a places it; the links among the institution's leaves set their order, and where they have none, they start together with the statute. Pay for a statutory leave that the institution does not grant as a leave of its own (waiting-week pay, a top-up, employer pay for FMLA leave) is linked `within` the statute; the statute is not linked `concurrent_with` its pay. An own leave that runs inside a statutory leave without starting with it is `concurrent_with` it, not `within` (R15's `within` starts together). | Old Dominion 2018 FMLA `concurrent_with` old_dominion_university_c1 (the change in duties). Dartmouth, maternity then parental leave with FMLA alongside → 22 (the birth parent's protected weeks are 26, set by the six-month medical leave) |
| S1a | Exception: where the statute or the institution's text makes bonding leave additional to leave for pregnancy or childbirth disability (e.g. California CFRA after PDL; Washington FLA; New Jersey FLA), the bonding leave is coded `consecutive_after` the disability leave component for the birth parent, with the textual basis in the note. Where no birth-only disability leave component exists, or the text does not say the leaves add, the bonding leave stays `concurrent_with` and the note says so. S1 still governs the bonding leave's relation to paid leave | Stanford CFRA `consecutive_after` PDL (handbook 3.5.3: "then eligible to receive an additional 12 weeks of bonding leave under CFRA") → birth protected 29.3; Portland State: Oregon parental leave is "in addition to" the 12 weeks of pregnancy-disability leave → `consecutive_after` |
| V4 | One component per mechanism or option. Bundles are split and linked with `sequence` (`in_lieu_of` for alternatives, `concurrent_with` for overlap). A stated benefit envelope that includes employer-paid waiting weeks ('95% of salary for up to 35 weeks, including the waiting period') stays one component, coded as written, not split by arithmetic the text does not state | Kansas State sick leave + FMLA; Southern California c14–c16 options (i)–(iii); George Mason full leave vs split; Victoria c4 pre-placement leave vs top-up. Saskatchewan 2018 supplement including the waiting weeks → one component |

## 5. Which provisions count: who, entitlement, scope, validity

| Id | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| R5 | The two derived roles are the birth parent ("woman" in v1.0) and the non-birth parent after a birth, i.e. the birth parent's partner ("man" in v1.0). Adoption, foster and surrogacy provisions are recorded but are not counted in either role. An intended parent through surrogacy is coded `surrogacy` (not `adoptive`); a surrogate who gives birth is a `birth_parent`. Where a provision covers "birth, including by surrogacy", `surrogacy` is listed with the roles the text names | Victoria adoption top-up → `adoptive`. Johns Hopkins parental leave ('parents using gestational carriers') → `surrogacy`, listed with the other roles. Memorial, "biological mothers, including surrogate mothers" → `birth_parent` |
| R6 | Caregiver conditions (primary, majority, equal) are gender-neutral | Southern California primary-caregiver leave counted for both roles |
| R12 | Every component records its entitlement (`guaranteed`, `guaranteed_on_request`, `discretionary`, `negotiated`). Derived figures are computed twice: guaranteed only (`guaranteed`, `guaranteed_on_request`), and including discretionary provisions (all four). `negotiated` means the benefit's amount or form is set by agreement with the chair or dean, with no guaranteed minimum; where the text gives the benefit and only its arrangements are agreed, it is `guaranteed_on_request` (V2). Where the amount or form is to be agreed with no guaranteed minimum, code `negotiated`, even where the grant itself is also at the unit's discretion. Refined by V2 | Claremont, Old Dominion. Colorado Boulder, "may seek to negotiate an alternative leave arrangement", granted by the needs of the unit → `negotiated`. A period the text grants, whose form is arranged with the chair or dean, is `guaranteed_on_request`. |
| V2 | Entitlement is decided by **what the approval decides**. If the text gives the faculty member the benefit ("may have", "is eligible for", "may elect") and the approval covers the arrangements (timing, coverage, the plan), code `guaranteed_on_request` (UC active service-modified duties, "eligible for a total period … of two quarters"). If the approval decides **whether** there is a benefit ("may be granted", "at the discretion of", "may request" with no grant language), code `discretionary`. Ambiguous cases are flagged | Chicago 2018 "the faculty member to have a one-quarter paid leave" + advance discussion of plans with the chair or dean → `guaranteed_on_request` |
| V2b | A benefit that "will/shall be granted unless" the institution makes a stated finding (undue burden, best interests) is coded `guaranteed_on_request`, because the default is to grant and refusal needs a specific finding. | V2c | Applies V2 to **advisory wording**: where a guideline or policy says the institution or a department head **"should"** arrange a benefit (rather than "shall", "will" or "is eligible for"), the approval decides whether there is a benefit, so code `discretionary`. It counts only in the figures that include discretionary provisions | Texas A&M 2007 Parental Teaching Load Redistribution guideline: a primary caregiver "would qualify", and department heads "should work with faculty to arrange one / two semester(s) of relief from formal classroom teaching" → discretionary (0 guaranteed; 16 weeks, Just teaching, with discretionary) |
| V2d | Where the text provides a benefit and states that requests are generally, normally or ordinarily granted, or will not be unreasonably denied, code `guaranteed_on_request` with the flag `conditional_refusal: <wording>`, as under V2b. Stays discretionary: Michigan State modified duties ("does not create an entitlement if there are legitimate business reasons for denying"). "Expected to accommodate requests" is V2d, not V2c (ASU ACD 710). |
| V3 | A provision counts as main campus if it covers the units that hold the institution's tenure-stream faculty in the arts and sciences (humanities, social sciences, natural sciences) and in engineering where the institution has it. Where arts and sciences are split into several faculties, all of them are needed. The scope is recorded in a coded field `faculty_scope` (e.g. "SAS and Hajim only") | Rochester 50% teaching exemption → main campus, `faculty_scope: "SAS and Hajim only"` |
| V3b | V3 applied strictly: a provision limited to a single college or faculty (one that does not cover both the arts-and-sciences and engineering units) is **not** main campus and is not counted. It stays in the record with `faculty_group` naming the unit and `faculty_scope` set, and the derived tables list every scope-limited leave or relief component, counted or not, in the column `scope_limits`, so partial coverage is visible downstream | Ohio State College of Arts and Sciences modified duties (one semester, "all tenured or tenure-eligible faculty members" of the College) → not counted; the figure is 6 (birth parent) / 3 (non-birth parent). UBC Faculty of Science reductions → not counted. Johns Hopkins Homewood (Krieger Arts and Sciences + Whiting Engineering) → main campus |
| V3c | Where a university-wide policy and a school- or department-level policy both cover main-campus faculty and differ, the **university-wide policy sets the institution's figures**; the unit-level provisions stay in the record, with `faculty_group` naming the unit and `faculty_scope` set, so they appear as not counted in `scope_limits`. "University-wide" means the institution in the sampling frame: the 205 U.S. and Canadian universities in v1.0 (v1.0's second row for the Polytechnic Institute of NYU, which repeated NYU's policy, is not kept). For a multi-campus system that is the campus: a campus-wide policy, including a provost's directive for the campus, is the institution's own policy. A system-wide policy also applies to the campus. Both are coded. Where they provide the same benefit, they are linked so that the more generous counts and neither adds to the other: `concurrent_with` when they run together, `in_lieu_of` when the parent chooses one. V3c applies where a system or university-wide policy and a campus or unit policy each grant a benefit; where two governing documents disagree about the same provision, V3d decides. | Johns Hopkins: the university Family Leave for New Parents policy (2017-07-01; 6 weeks birth recovery + 4 weeks parental) governs; the Homewood Schools faculty FML policy is recorded but not counted → 10 / 4. Colorado Boulder: the 2007 Provost memo (up to one semester) and CU system APS 5019 (18 weeks), `concurrent_with` |
| V3d | Where two governing documents in force on the reference date disagree about a provision, the reading is decided in this order: (1) a precedence clause in either document; (2) for members of a bargaining unit, the collective agreement over a handbook, policy or HR rule; (3) the higher authority over the document implementing it (a board, regents' or system policy over a campus or unit document; V3c settles campus-wide versus unit-level texts); (4) the later-adopted document over the earlier; (5) the faculty-specific text over a general employee policy. The other reading is kept in a `conflicting_sources` flag. A passage in a governing document that restates another policy and defers to it is a summary of that policy (source precedence, below) | Colorado Boulder: the 2018 campus pages over the 2007 provost memo for the one-semester paid leave |
| V5 | Provisions not yet in effect on the data set's reference date are kept in the record, flagged `not_yet_effective: <date>`, and excluded from derived values | Ohio State Parental Care Guidebook revised 10/01/18 (`not_yet_effective: 2018-10-01`) |

**Source precedence:** where a summary or FAQ and the governing document in force on the reference date (handbook, agreement, policy) conflict, the governing document decides (Rochester: Faculty Handbook May 2017 over the undated FAQ). A provision found only in a summary or FAQ, and not in the governing document, is recorded, flagged `summary_only: <doc_id>`, and counted in no figure (Virginia Tech 2018: 10 days of paid family leave on the undated HR page only; Alberta 2018: unpaid leave stated only in the HR tipsheet). A summary may still supply a detail of a provision the governing document contains (V1b). Within a document, a provision is read with its implementing paragraphs, definitions and exceptions; a specific provision governs a general statement of the same benefit (Oakland 2018: 'shall be granted upon approval' in ¶173, 'may be granted' with grounds for rejection in ¶175–177 → `discretionary`).

|---|---|---|
| U1 | General-purpose leaves, paid or unpaid, that parents may also use but that exist for any purpose (including one that names child care among its reasons) are kept in the record, coded by what they are (`employer_paid_leave`, `unpaid_leave`), flagged `general_purpose`, and counted in no figure. A leave that exists only because of a birth or adoption is not general. General-purpose means available for reasons other than health, family care or a birth or adoption. A list of purposes that is not exhaustive ('such as', 'including', 'for example', 'exceptional circumstances') does not limit the provision to the purposes it names. A closed list whose grounds are all birth, adoption, health, family care or a death in the family is not general-purpose. The provision's current purpose governs, not an earlier title. | U2 | Where the documents name a statute but not its length, the length comes from the statute itself.
